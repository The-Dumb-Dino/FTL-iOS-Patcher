FTL iOS Patcher

Patches FTL for iPad 1.6.21 for newer iPhones and iPads, with saves in the Files app and the game’s log.

Advanced

Shown under the icon on the home screen.

A square PNG or macOS .icns; leave empty to keep the game’s icon.

Use another one (like com.example.ftl) to keep it next to a second copy on the device.

The patcher includes the game’s prepatch. Give another .ftlt only if your game or Hyperspace build needs it.

Hyperspace isn’t included. Use Hyperspace.1.6.21.arm64.dylib from its iOS build. If your build has other hooks than this release’s, also give its prepatch-hyperspace.ftlt above.

Save the patched IPA

Install it with Sideloadly, AltStore or SideStore, like any IPA.

    What it needs

    • A decrypted FTL 1.6.21 IPA (the App Store’s is encrypted).
    • iOS 14 or newer for the patched app.
    • For mods: an IPA that already has them in its ftl.dat, and Hyperspace’s iOS build if they use Hyperspace.

    What it does

    It patches the game’s binary and Info.plist the same way the desktop patcher does, and adds ftlios.dylib. In Advanced you can change the name and icon, use another bundle ID, or add Hyperspace. The mods’ ftl.dat is copied as it is. Everything happens in this tab: the IPA is never uploaded, and the patched one is put together in memory and on your disk.
